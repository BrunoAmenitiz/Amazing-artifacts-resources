Three more questions that get you a story

  • "Direct share last year, and two years ago?" The direction tells you more than the number.
  • "Who changed your prices last, and when?" Reveals a revenue function, or a PriceLabs licence nobody uses.
  • "Who sends the guest registrations, and what happens when it's late?" Admin team, accountant, or the owner at midnight.

The one pain everyone shares

Nobody wants trouble with the authorities. Police registration, tourist tax, the statistical return and certified invoices come up in every segment.

A franchise or brand may already have chosen the PMS. Nothing in the product changes that, so find out early.

Stage 01 · Fill my rooms

Every direct booking is the full price and a guest the hotel owns.

An OTA booking costs 15–25% commission and the guest belongs to the OTA. Show it as one flow: get found with AmenitizBoost, convince with the website, convert with the booking engine.

01

What to show

  • Website. A module on top of Core and Advanced. Responsive, built to Google norms, page builder, AI-generated SEO texts, blog and custom domain. Edited without a developer.
  • Google Hotel free links. The direct price on Google Search and Maps next to the OTAs, no commission. Needs a Google My Business sheet, the same name and address as in Amenitiz, an eligible property type and all direct rates active.
  • AmenitizBoost. Fully managed ads on Google Search, Google Hotel Ads and Microsoft Ads. No setup, no ad spend, no agency fee: 12% only on confirmed bookings it brings.

 

  • Booking engine. Book and pay in three clicks, with rate plans, policies, promotions, extras and gift cards.
  • Channel control. Room-type inventory control lets the hotel decide what each channel gets, for example some rooms on the website only.
  • Rates. Derive the grid from the standard rate, so one price change moves every rate.
  • PriceLabs. Two-way: we send booking data, PriceLabs sends prices back into the rates.
Say

"With AmenitizBoost, no bookings, no cost: you only pay 12% on what Boost brings."

"Room Link derives one level: we set up your most popular combination and you block the others by hand."

Don't say

"You'll get at least 20% more bookings." Nobody can guarantee results.

"Room Link handles every combination of your cottages."

🥊
Toughest question

"Can you guarantee more bookings?"

No. But Boost costs nothing until a booking comes in: no ad spend, no marketing skills needed, no risk.

Stage 02 · Secure the booking & get paid

The money arrives, even when the guest doesn't.

AmenitizPay: 1.4% + €0.25 per transaction, no commission on direct bookings, no per-booking fee, no activation fee. Open the demo with Stripe Express onboarding: about a minute, and it answers what the identity check asks for and when money lands.

02

What to show

  • Pre-authorisations, held up to 30 days. Once released, a hold can't be captured again, so a late charge needs a fresh payment.
  • Automatic payment schedules, for example 75% / 25%, up to three charges. They run on their own once set.
  • Payment links for anything outside the schedule.
  • Daily payout, €9.36 a month + VAT, for hotels worried about cash flow.

Objections you'll hear

  • Payouts are too slow for my cash flow.
  • The fee is higher than my bank's terminal.
  • I want to keep my own gateway or terminal.
Say

"The fee is the fee. Here's what it pays for: recovered no-shows, no reconciliation, no commission on direct."

"OTA virtual cards can't be pre-authorised, and Airbnb payments can't be automated."

Don't say

Any discount. Change the conversation, not the price.

That every payment can be automated.

🥊
Toughest question

"My bank charges me 0.8%. Why pay you more?"

Reframe on total cost: no-shows recovered, time saved matching receipts to bookings, and no commission on direct bookings.

WhatsApp, explained honestly

  • Free-text messages cost €0.02 each, and the first 1,000 a month are free.
  • More than 24 hours after the guest's last message, the hotel needs a template. Meta bills templates directly, not Amenitiz.
  • Templates can be scheduled, for example the Chekin check-in link before arrival.
  • To connect: a Meta Business account with admin access, a number that can receive a code, an approved display name and a payment method on Meta. Check what's on that number today first.

Ami: match the level to the hotelier

  • Manual. The team clicks Generate, checks, edits and sends. For "wants to be in it".
  • Auto-suggest. The reply is ready; the team decides. For "wants to be in it, but stretched".
  • Auto. Ami replies on its own, even at 2am, signed "Sent by AI". For "wants it handled".

How invoicing works

  • The compliant invoice for a stay is issued manually, on the check-out day.
  • Ground every answer in invoices and series, and in proforma versus invoice.
  • Accounting software: no direct connection today, but it will be possible soon. Until then, the hotel can export its accounting entries as a file for its accountant.

Escalate, don't improvise

  • Registratore di cassa
  • Multi-account Veri*Factu
  • Autónomos
  • Properties without a partita IVA

Objections you'll hear

  • "Your invoicing can't handle my setup": several VAT rates on one bill, split B2B and B2C, refunds and credit notes, deposits kept apart, self-invoices for foreign companies, choosing who the invoice is addressed to.
  • "It's too manual, and not integrated": anything done outside Amenitiz, like sending e-invoices, an Excel summary for the accountant or uploads to government portals.

Stage 07 · Know how my business is doing

Show the numbers the owner already asks for.

The "wants the numbers" hotelier opens with them: the channel split, what Booking.com really costs, and RevPAR against last year.

07

Read the two numbers right

  • ADR (average daily rate) = room revenue ÷ rooms actually sold. The average price a sold room went for.
  • RevPAR = room revenue ÷ every room available, sold or not.
  • ADR up while total revenue goes down usually means occupancy has dropped.

No forecast today

There's no pace forecast. Show the comparison with the same period last year instead.

Say

"There's no forecast, but you can compare this period with last year."

Don't say

"The pace report forecasts where you'll land next month."

Traps to avoid

These sound right and lose trust when the customer finds out. An honest "I'll confirm that for you" beats a confident wrong answer.

"Room Link handles every combination."

Room Link derives one level only. Set up the most popular combination and block the others by hand.

"We automate all your payments."

OTA virtual cards can't be pre-authorised, and Airbnb payments can't be automated.

"Capture the hold whenever you need."

Once a pre-authorisation is released, it can't be captured. A late charge needs a fresh payment.

"WhatsApp is free within 24 hours."

Free-text messages cost €0.02 each after the first 1,000 a month. Templates are billed by Meta.

"Ami translates guest messages."

Translations are on the roadmap, not live.

"You can answer emails from the inbox."

Email is imminent, not live.

"Expedia messages land in the inbox."

Expedia is on the roadmap.

"Every guest gets a door code."

Only if the hotel's lock is supported.

"Your restaurant POS works as a payment method."

It doesn't. Charge it to a room booking, or mark it as paid outside Amenitiz.

"Amenitiz POS works in your country."

It's certified and sold in Spain and Portugal only.

"We'll integrate your till in a few months."

We don't have it today. We can study it, but can't promise.

"The invoice goes out automatically."

The compliant invoice is issued manually, on the check-out day.

"We sync with Pennylane."

Not today, but it will be possible soon. Until then, the hotel exports a file for its accountant.

"The pace report forecasts next month."

There's no forecast. Compare with the same period last year.

"You'll get 20% more bookings."

No guarantee. Boost costs nothing until a booking comes in.
